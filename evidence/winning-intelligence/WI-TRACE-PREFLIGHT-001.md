## Canonical product truth preserved

Skeptara remains:

> **Independent counter-evidence before autonomous execution.**

Hero vertical: autonomous GitHub dependency-change merge.

Protected mechanism:

`PR proposed -> deterministic external risk tier -> risk-proportional paid Telegraph counter-evidence -> deterministic PASS/BLOCK/ESCALATE -> exact-head protected GitHub execution`

The proposer does not score its own risk and does not audit itself. Only a fresh exact PASS may unlock a bounded protected action.

## Winning Intelligence verdict

**The product mechanism is strong enough to remain frozen. Do not add a new core feature.**

The remaining winning work is presentation, judge comprehension, proof visibility, activity/usage packaging, and clean submission execution.

### Biggest strength

Skeptara is not merely a "verified intelligence" consumer and not merely a confidence threshold around an action. Its distinctive mechanism is **paid adversarial counter-evidence whose depth and budget scale with externally assigned downside, with fail-closed execution binding to the exact action artifact.**

### Biggest strategic risk

The generic story "verified intelligence -> governed action" is now crowded enough to be dangerous. A current public Telegraph Track 3 competitor, Cognivern, presents itself as production-ready and explicitly claims confidence-gated Telegraph calls, budget-enforced x402 spend, audit artifacts, attribution, and governed execution. Therefore Skeptara must **not** lead with generic governance, confidence gating, x402 payments, or auditability as its distinction.

### Distinction to defend

Lead with the mechanism competitors do not visibly match:

1. **External downside scoring** — the reviewed agent cannot shrink its own scrutiny.
2. **Risk -> evidence depth + spend** — LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH determines how much independent evidence must be bought.
3. **Counter-evidence, not confirmation** — Telegraph is used to actively seek reasons the proposed action should *not* execute.
4. **Asymmetric stopping** — one material finding can BLOCK immediately; incomplete evidence never becomes PASS.
5. **Exact execution binding** — PASS is bound to repository, PR, head SHA, action fingerprint and freshness.
6. **Two real outcomes proven** — challenged PR #1 BLOCKed and could not execute; clean PR #2 PASSed and was really merged only after exact-head revalidation and explicit bounded human authorization.
