# Skeptara Constitution

Status: DERIVED READY — reconciled to approved `product/PRD.md` v0.1

## I. Product authority

The living PRD is the canonical product-intent source. Spec Kit artifacts may clarify and operationalize approved intent but may not silently redefine it. Material discoveries route back to the PRD before derived artifacts are reconciled.

## II. Fail closed

No incomplete, stale, ambiguous, unbound, or insufficiently challenged action may become `PASS`. Budget exhaustion before required challenge coverage is `ESCALATE/BLOCK`, never success.

## III. Separation of duties

The reviewed coding agent may propose an action but may not:
- assign its own final risk tier;
- declare its own challenge complete;
- set final gate outcome;
- bypass the independent auditor.

## IV. Real evidence over simulated claims

Fixtures are allowed only for deterministic local tests/UI development and must be labeled as fixtures. Submission-path intelligence must use real Telegraph miners. No fake receipts, hashes, miner scores, provenance, or settlement metadata.

## V. Protected actions are bounded

GitHub writes are limited to allow-listed demo repositories/PRs. Secrets never enter browser code, commits, public logs, or chat. A merge can execute only against the exact reviewed head SHA with fresh PASS evidence.

## VI. One vertical before generalization

The hackathon MVP proves the mechanism on autonomous dependency-change merges. Generic middleware, treasury governance, insurance, formal VoI optimization, and universal warrant standards are out of scope.

## VII. Evidence before promotion

READY, BUILD_CANDIDATE, and COMPLETE are distinct states. Demo success is not production evidence. TRACE may assess design/judge path when triggered; Project Finisher owns terminal assurance before final submission claims.

## VIII. Historical traceability

Canonical state, handover, PRD revisions, material decisions, gate results, and evidence references must remain traceable in GitHub so a new conversation/runtime can resume from repository truth rather than chat memory.
